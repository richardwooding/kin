Where to order copies

The archive references on this page are index entries. The files themselves are held as below and copies are supplied on request, quoting the full reference, for example "KAB MOOC 6/9/17813 ref 6448/50".

Reference prefixHoldingHow to order
KAB …Cape Town Archives Repository, 72 Roeland Street, Cape Town. Cape estate files (MOOC), Supreme Court cases (CSC), deeds (DOC), Attorney-General and Treasury papers (AG, T).Written request to the repository with the reference; a photocopy or scan fee applies. Contact details are on the National Archives site under Contact information. Researchers may also read files in the reading room.
TAB …National Archives Repository, 24 Hamilton Street, Arcadia, Pretoria. Transvaal estate files (MHG), Witwatersrand court cases (WLD), Transvaal government papers.Same procedure, addressed to the Pretoria repository. Estate files usually open with the death notice; ask for that page if the whole file is not needed.
TBE …Port Elizabeth Archives Repository. Eastern Cape court and local records (GSEE).Same procedure, addressed to Port Elizabeth.
FamilySearch imagesCape probate 1822 to 1990 and Transvaal probate 1869 to 1961 are photographed and free to view online, so files in those ranges need not be ordered at all; only the Anglican register images are restricted, and those can be viewed free at any FamilySearch centre or affiliate library.Open the record page and step through the film; the folder and image numbers on this page locate each entry.
EnglandBirth, marriage and death entries: General Register Office, gro.gov.uk, digital images for a fee. Cornish parish registers: Kresen Kernow, the Cornwall archive at Redruth; the free Online Parish Clerks transcriptions used here cite the originals held there.GRO orders are placed from the index row; Kresen Kernow supplies copies of register pages on request.
