Where to order copies

Archive references above (prefixed KAB, TAB or TBE) are index entries from NAAIRS, the National Archives of South Africa's finding aid. Copies of the files are supplied on written request, quoting the full reference:

  • KAB: Cape Town Archives Repository, 72 Roeland Street, Cape Town. Estate files (MOOC), Supreme Court cases (CSC), deeds (DOC) and government papers (AG, T).
  • TAB: National Archives Repository, 24 Hamilton Street, Arcadia, Pretoria. Transvaal estate files (MHG) and Witwatersrand court cases (WLD).
  • TBE: Port Elizabeth Archives Repository. Eastern Cape court records (GSEE).
  • Estates too recent for the index are held by the Master of the High Court for the district of death; a descendant may request the death notice and account.
  • Cape probate files 1822 to 1990 and Transvaal probate files 1869 to 1961 are also photographed on FamilySearch and can be read there free; only the Anglican register images are restricted to FamilySearch centres.
  • English entries: General Register Office (gro.gov.uk) for civil registration images; Kresen Kernow, Redruth, for Cornish parish registers.

Contact details for the South African repositories are on the National Archives site, national.archives.gov.za, under Contact information.
